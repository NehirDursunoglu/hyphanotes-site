How it works

Capture now. Connect later. Understand why.

Most notes are written once and never read again. Hypha keeps them working: every new thought is a chance for an old one to become useful.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Type, speak, or photograph a page. There are no folders to choose and nothing to set up first. The app opens straight into writing.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Hypha reads the new note against your whole history and shortlists the few notes that genuinely help, not the ones that merely share a word.

  3. 03

    Understand

    Each connection states its reason in a single line, so you know in a glance whether it is a past solution, an example, or a problem returning.

Precision over volume

Fewer links. Better links.

A notes app that links everything to everything is noise. Hypha is deliberately strict about what it shows you.

Meaning, not keywords

Two notes that both mention coffee are not connected. A link appears only when the older note changes what you would do next.

Checked twice

Every proposed connection is reviewed a second time on its own before you see it. Weak or invented reasons are dropped.

Across languages

Write in Turkish, Spanish or English. A note in one language can bring back a note you wrote in another.

You stay in charge

If a connection is wrong, remove it with one tap. Hypha will not suggest those two notes together again.

Privacy

Your notes stay yours.

  • No ads and no trackers. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools, and your data is never sold.
  • Not used to train AI. Notes are processed through the OpenAI API, whose terms exclude training on this data.
  • AI is optional. Turn connections off and nothing is sent for processing.
  • Leave whenever you like. Delete your account inside the app and everything is removed from our servers.
